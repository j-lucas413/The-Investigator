# Ransomware Incident Report

## Summary
A ransomware incident occurred on May 2nd, 2026, affecting multiple hosts and users within our organization. The incident involved a phishing attack that exploited a vulnerability in our VPN service, allowing attackers to gain initial access. The attackers then moved laterally across the network, encrypting sensitive files and demanding ransom payments. Our Incident Response Team (IRT) was activated promptly, and we implemented containment, eradication, and recovery measures to mitigate the impact of the incident.

## Root Cause
The root cause of the incident is attributed to a phishing attack that exploited a vulnerability in our VPN service. The attackers were able to gain initial access and move laterally across the network, ultimately encrypting sensitive files and demanding ransom payments.
