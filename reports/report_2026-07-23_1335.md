**Incident Report**

## Summary
On 2026-06-12, an unauthorized user logged into SRV-RDP-01 using a compromised credential (hadmin) from 91.219.236.18. This resulted in malicious activities such as creating a new PowerShell process that disabled real-time monitoring and compressing financial data for ransom. The attack vector was exploited through a phishing or exposed RDP, with a timeline of multiple failed logon attempts before the successful login.

The root cause of the incident is an initial access vector through a compromised credential, followed by lateral movement and malicious activities.
